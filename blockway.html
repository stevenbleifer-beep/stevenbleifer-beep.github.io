Blockway Maps

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Blockway does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Location. Blockway uses your location on your iPhone to show where you are on the map and to give driving directions. Your location is not sent to the developer.

Maps, search and directions. Map imagery, place search and routes come from Apple Maps. When you search or ask for a route, that request is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Data kept on your phone. Map areas you have viewed are saved on your iPhone so they load faster next time. Deleting the app removes them.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
